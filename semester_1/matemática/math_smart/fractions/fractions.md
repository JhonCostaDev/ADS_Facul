## The Basics

A fraction is made of two numbers stacked on top of each other with a line in the middle.

            3 / 4 => 3
                    ---
                     4

    * The bottom number (called the denomator) - Tells us the total number of equals pieces the whole thing has been cut into.

    * The top number (called the numerator) - Tells us how many of those pieces we are talking about or have left.

## Equivalent Fractions

Equivalent fractions are fractions that look different but actually show the exact same amount.

for example: You cur the first pizza into 2 equal slices and eat 1 slice. You ate 1/2 of the pizza.

             You cut the second pizza into 4 equal slices and eat 2 slices. You ate 2/4 of the pizza.

To change a fraction into an equivalent one, you just multiply or divide the top and the bottom by the same number.

1   2    2
- x - = --- 
2   2    4

1/2 is equivalent to 2/4, they look different, but they all represent the exact same half-size position.

## Fractions Operationss

* Fractions with the same bottom or denominator: When fractions have the same bottom number (denominator), adding and subtrating are the same operation like adding and subtrating decimals numbers

1   2   3
- + - = -
5   5   5


8   3   5
- - - = -
10  10  10

* Fractions with different bottom (denominator): When fractions have different bottom (denominator), we need to make the pieces the same size by finding a common denominator.

If we add 1/2 + 1/4, first we need to find the equivalent fraction of 1/2 that have the same denominator to 1/4. We do that multiplying 1/2 by 2 and get 2/4, now we can add them.

                find the equivalent fraction
                    1   2   2
                    - x - = -
                    2   2   4

                adding
                    2   1   3
                    - + - = -
                    4   4   4

so, 1/2 + 1/4 it's the same that 3/4
